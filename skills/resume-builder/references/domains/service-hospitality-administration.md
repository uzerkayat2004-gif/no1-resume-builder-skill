# Retail, hospitality, food service, customer service, and administration

## Evidence to prioritize

- customer/guest service, shift, store, front desk, food, facilities, scheduling, records, communications, or office support;
- reliability, accuracy, hygiene, safety, speed, sales, repeat business, discretion, teamwork, and training;
- responsibilities during busy, sensitive, or high-volume periods;
- systems, cash handling, inventory, reservations, records, or coordination tools.

## Questions

- What did customers, guests, colleagues, or managers rely on you to handle?
- What happened during a difficult or busy situation?
- What process, service, schedule, record, or space did you keep accurate and organized?
- How did you improve service, safety, speed, sales, or reliability?
- Did you train others, open/close, handle cash, manage stock, or protect confidential information?

## Useful sections

Summary only when targeted; Skills; Experience; Certifications; Education.

## Cautions

- Do not invent sales, satisfaction, volume, or cash figures.
- Reliability and service judgment are valid evidence even without metrics.
- Do not hide informal or part-time work; label it accurately.
- Keep personal customer and employer information private.
