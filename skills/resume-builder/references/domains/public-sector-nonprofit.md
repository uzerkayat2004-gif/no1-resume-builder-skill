# Government, public sector, nonprofit, and social impact

## Evidence to prioritize

- public service, statutory responsibility, policy, program, casework, community need, funding, partnerships, or operations;
- access, stewardship, compliance, timeliness, participation, trust, service quality, sustainability, or beneficiary outcomes;
- scope across geography, population, caseload, budget, partners, or staff when verified;
- equity and inclusion described through actual actions and outcomes.

## Questions

- What public or community need did the work address?
- What service, policy, program, case, campaign, or partnership did you support?
- Which regulations, funder requirements, stakeholder groups, or constraints mattered?
- What did you personally decide, deliver, coordinate, or improve?
- What shows responsible use of resources and meaningful service?

## Useful sections

Summary; Core Capabilities; Experience; Programs/Policy/Community Projects; Education; Certifications/Languages.

## Cautions

- Government applications may require specialized formats and fields.
- Do not expose case, beneficiary, security, or politically sensitive details.
- Avoid savior language and unverified impact attribution.
- Distinguish funds raised, managed, influenced, and awarded.
