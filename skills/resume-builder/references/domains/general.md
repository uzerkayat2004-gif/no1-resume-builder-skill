# General and emerging occupations

Use when no specific profile fits.

## Discover the work

Ask:

- What does the person, customer, organization, or community rely on you to deliver?
- What problems or risks appear repeatedly?
- What tools, equipment, systems, standards, knowledge, or judgment matter?
- What distinguishes safe, reliable, high-quality, or excellent work?
- What became better because of your contribution?

## Evidence

Accept verified evidence of:

- completed work or deliverables;
- quality, reliability, safety, or timeliness;
- customer or stakeholder trust;
- complexity or constraints handled;
- learning, certifications, or demonstrated practice;
- recognition, repeat work, responsibility, or leadership.

## Structure

Use the sections that prove fit. Do not force a technical-skills section, metrics, or a corporate summary onto work where they add no value.
