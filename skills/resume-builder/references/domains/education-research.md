# Education, training, academia, and research

## Evidence to prioritize

- learner population, subject, modality, curriculum, assessment, inclusion, advising, or program design;
- research questions, methods, datasets, experiments, publications, grants, presentations, reproducibility, and collaboration;
- learner progress, program quality, teaching practice, scholarly contribution, or research impact;
- verified authorship, publication status, funding role, and credentials.

## Questions

- Who learned or benefited, and what was the goal?
- What did you design, teach, assess, investigate, analyze, publish, or improve?
- Which pedagogy, method, dataset, instrument, or framework did you use?
- What evidence shows learning, inclusion, rigor, reproducibility, or contribution?
- What is your exact authorship, grant, and teaching role?

## Useful sections

Education; Appointments/Experience; Teaching; Research; Publications; Grants; Presentations; Methods/Skills; Service.

For ordinary education roles, use a targeted resume or CV as requested. For academic applications, use the academic regional profile and do not impose a private-sector page limit.

## Cautions

- Distinguish submitted, under review, accepted, in press, and published.
- Do not inflate authorship order, grant ownership, learner outcomes, or institutional impact.
- Respect student and research-participant privacy.
