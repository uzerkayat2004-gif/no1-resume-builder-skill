# Healthcare, social care, public health, pharma, biotech, and life sciences

## Evidence to prioritize

- patient/client population, setting, acuity, caseload, service, study, laboratory, product, or quality system;
- safe care, coordination, documentation, education, access, continuity, prevention, reproducibility, or regulated milestones;
- licenses, registrations, certifications, methods, and standards;
- teamwork and escalation within the candidate's scope of practice.

## Questions

- What population, setting, study, product, or scientific problem did you support?
- What was your licensed or assigned scope?
- What did you assess, deliver, coordinate, document, test, improve, or escalate?
- Which standards, protocols, methods, systems, or quality controls applied?
- What result can be stated without exposing protected information?

## Useful sections

Licensure/Registration near the top when required; Summary; Clinical/Research/Industry Experience; Skills/Methods; Education; Certifications; Publications when relevant.

## Cautions

- Never include patient-identifying information.
- Do not imply a license, specialty, procedure, authorship, or clinical authority.
- Avoid causal claims about health outcomes unless supported and appropriately scoped.
- Verify country-specific licensing and application requirements from official bodies.
