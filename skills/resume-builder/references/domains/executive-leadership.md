# Executive, founder, and enterprise leadership

## Evidence to prioritize

- enterprise or portfolio scope;
- strategy and capital allocation;
- operating model and transformation;
- revenue, margin, cash, risk, governance, customers, products, or geographic expansion when confirmed;
- leadership-team building, succession, culture, and organizational capability;
- board, investor, regulator, partner, or community relationships;
- durable outcomes and difficult trade-offs.

## Questions

- What enterprise decision or inflection point did you lead?
- What was the verified scope: organization, business unit, geography, budget, workforce, or portfolio?
- What choices did you personally make, and what was delegated?
- How did the operating model, leadership system, or organizational capability change?
- Which outcomes lasted, and what external factors also contributed?

## Useful sections

Executive Summary; Leadership Scope/Capabilities; Selected Experience; Board/Advisory; Education; relevant Recognition.

Use two pages when the relevant evidence needs it. Add a separate board bio, deal sheet, or leadership narrative only when the application context calls for one.

## Cautions

- Do not attribute all company performance to one leader.
- Separate organic growth, acquisition effects, market movement, and direct contribution.
- Do not copy celebrity-resume conventions.
- Protect confidential strategy, compensation, board, investor, and transaction details.
