# Finance, banking, consulting, legal, compliance, and policy

## Evidence to prioritize

- decisions, transactions, analyses, controls, forecasts, closes, audits, matters, contracts, policies, or transformations;
- verified scale: budget, portfolio, transaction, caseload, jurisdiction, or business unit;
- accuracy, risk, timeliness, savings, compliance, negotiation, governance, or implementation;
- models, methods, regulations, stakeholders, and professional judgment.

## Questions

- What decision, matter, transaction, risk, or client problem did the work address?
- What analysis, model, diligence, negotiation, control, or recommendation did you own?
- What was the verified scope and jurisdiction?
- What changed or was protected because of the work?
- What can be described without breaching confidentiality or privilege?

## Useful sections

Summary; Experience; Selected Transactions/Engagements/Matters only when appropriate; Technical/Professional Skills; Education; Licenses/Certifications.

## Cautions

- Never invent deal size, savings, audit results, or legal outcomes.
- Distinguish supported, advised, managed, and led.
- Keep client-confidential and privileged details out.
- Confirm credential and jurisdiction requirements.
- Interest lines and deal sheets are context-specific, not universal.
