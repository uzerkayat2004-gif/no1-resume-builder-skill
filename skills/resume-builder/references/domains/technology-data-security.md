# Technology, data, AI, cybersecurity, and IT

## Evidence to prioritize

- systems, products, models, analyses, or environments changed;
- scale and constraints only when confirmed;
- architecture, data quality, reliability, latency, security, cost, accessibility, or developer/user experience;
- decision or operational impact;
- incident, migration, automation, experimentation, or delivery ownership;
- collaboration across engineering, product, design, security, or operations.

## Questions

- What was the system or decision before your work?
- What did you personally design, implement, analyze, debug, migrate, secure, or automate?
- Which tools and methods were important, and why?
- What trade-offs, failures, incidents, or constraints did you handle?
- How was quality or impact observed?

## Useful sections

Summary when targeted; Skills; Experience; Selected Projects; Education/Certifications.

For data and AI, distinguish analysis, engineering, modeling, evaluation, deployment, and business decision support. For security, distinguish monitoring, control design, response, governance, testing, and remediation.

## Cautions

- Do not infer scale from company size.
- Do not list every technology mentioned in the posting.
- Do not expose secrets, vulnerabilities, customer data, or proprietary architecture.
- Do not equate course projects with production experience.
- Avoid unsupported performance percentages and “architected” language when ownership was narrower.
