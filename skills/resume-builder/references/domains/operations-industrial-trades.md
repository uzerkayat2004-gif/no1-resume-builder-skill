# Operations, supply chain, engineering, manufacturing, construction, and trades

## Evidence to prioritize

- processes, equipment, facilities, sites, inventory, suppliers, schedules, fleets, orders, or field service;
- safety, quality, throughput, yield, downtime, delivery, reliability, waste, cost, diagnostics, or customer outcome;
- licenses, standards, methods, tools, equipment, materials, and inspections;
- team, shift, site, project, or geographic scope when confirmed.

## Questions

- What process, asset, project, or service did you keep running or improve?
- What failure, bottleneck, defect, delay, safety risk, or customer problem did you solve?
- Which equipment, drawings, standards, tools, or methods did you use?
- What was your exact responsibility on the team or site?
- What evidence shows safe, reliable, timely, or high-quality work?

## Useful sections

Summary when useful; Licenses/Certifications; Skills/Equipment; Experience; Selected Projects; Education/Apprenticeship.

## Cautions

- Safety claims must be accurate and scoped.
- Do not imply licenses, trade tickets, clearances, or sign-off authority.
- Avoid disclosing controlled site or infrastructure details.
- Do not make office-style metrics the only evidence; workmanship and reliability matter.
