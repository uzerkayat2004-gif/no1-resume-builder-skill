# Marketing, communications, sales, and customer success

## Evidence to prioritize

- market, audience, segment, territory, account type, or customer lifecycle;
- campaign, message, channel, sales motion, partnership, adoption, retention, or support responsibility;
- qualified demand, revenue, pipeline, conversion, expansion, renewal, satisfaction, resolution, or reputation when confirmed;
- customer insight, relationship quality, negotiation, enablement, and process improvement.

## Questions

- Who was the audience or customer, and what did they need?
- What did you personally create, sell, negotiate, communicate, or improve?
- Which channels, systems, methods, or playbooks did you use?
- What evidence shows quality or movement through the funnel or lifecycle?
- What did you learn and change?

## Useful sections

Targeted Summary; Core Capabilities; Experience; Campaigns/Accounts/Projects when needed; Education/Certifications.

## Cautions

- Clarify sourced versus influenced revenue.
- Distinguish bookings, pipeline, reach, impressions, engagement, conversion, retention, and satisfaction.
- Do not expose client names, quotas, pricing, or confidential account data without permission.
- Do not inflate a team campaign into sole ownership.
