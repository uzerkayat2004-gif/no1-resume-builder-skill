# India profile

Use for private-sector applications in India unless the employer, campus process, or government form says otherwise.

- A4 is the default paper size.
- Use English when the posting is in English; otherwise match the required language.
- Include city/state, phone, email, and relevant professional links.
- Do not include date of birth, religion, marital status, government ID, full address, declaration, signature, or photo by default.
- For students and fresh graduates, Class X/XII details may be relevant in campus hiring or when the application explicitly asks; they are not universal resume requirements.
- Keep internships, substantial projects, hackathons, and relevant coursework when they provide the strongest evidence.
- Use one or two pages according to evidence and application instructions.

India has varied employer and sector conventions. Do not present a single private-sector convention as a legal rule.
