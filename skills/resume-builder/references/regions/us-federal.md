# United States federal profile

Use only for U.S. federal applications.

USAJOBS states that federal resumes uploaded to the system must be no more than two pages and should include information requested in the announcement. Federal resumes may require details that are uncommon in private-sector resumes, such as hours per week, series/grade, supervisor information, or specific eligibility evidence.

Workflow:

1. Read the entire announcement and questionnaire.
2. Capture every required field and document.
3. Map specialized-experience language to verified evidence.
4. Follow the current USAJOBS two-page requirement.
5. Do not reuse a private-sector one-page template without checking required detail.

Source: https://help.usajobs.gov/faq/application/documents/resume/what-to-include
