# Academic CV profile

Use for faculty, postdoctoral, research, and other applications that request an academic CV.

Do not impose the private-sector one- or two-page range. Organize evidence by the norms of the discipline and the application:

- research interests or profile;
- appointments;
- education;
- peer-reviewed publications and preprints, clearly distinguished;
- grants and fellowships with role and amount only when verified and appropriate;
- teaching;
- presentations;
- service;
- awards;
- methods, languages, or technical skills;
- references when requested.

Keep citation style consistent. Do not inflate authorship, publication status, funding role, or contribution. Follow the institution's requested format, including Europass or photo instructions when explicitly stated and knowingly accepted by the candidate.
