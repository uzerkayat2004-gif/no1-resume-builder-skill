# United States and Canada profile

For ordinary private-sector applications:

- Letter paper is the default.
- Do not include a photo, age/date of birth, marital/family status, religion, or government identification.
- City and state/province are normally sufficient; omit street address.
- One or two pages according to relevant evidence.
- Use month/year dates consistently.
- Keep a separate federal profile for USAJOBS applications.

Employer instructions and role-specific regulated requirements override this profile.
